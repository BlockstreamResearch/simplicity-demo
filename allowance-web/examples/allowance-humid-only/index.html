Allowance covenant Humid only

The same contract as the sibling allowance-lwk demo, driven entirely through the Humid extension. No wasm, no build step, no toolchain: one static HTML file plus the contract and its manifest. Humid does all the compiling, signing and broadcasting; this page only says what to do and keeps track of where the money is. Liquid testnet only.

The steps run in order, top to bottom. The beneficiary does 1 and 2 and sends their key over; the benefactor does 3, 4 and 5 and sends the completed terms back; the beneficiary does 6 and 7. Nothing asks you to scroll back up: the covenant address only exists after funding, so the handoff is 5 and receiving it is 6.

One Humid wallet can play both roles, in which case you can skip 5 and 6 entirely and just keep going. Two browser profiles, each with its own Humid, is the realistic two-party test.

The contract

Shared by both roles and filled in for you: funding writes them, watching reads them. Paste either by hand if you were told it rather than sent a terms blob.

Humid needs the patch at ../allowance-humid/humid-allowance-fixes.patch applied and rebuilt; without it every withdrawal is refused as unimplemented-construct, and FullWithdraw fails separately as “pays nothing”. Every action shows Humid's own “Perform a contract action?” confirmation: that is where you approve each transaction.

1 · Humid

Checks the extension is available on this page, and whether a session already exists. Reading the session does not prompt; creating one does, so this page only creates one when a call is actually refused.

2 · Your keybeneficiary

Humid shows your public key nowhere, so derive it from the descriptor it signs with: a BIP84 xpub already at m/84'/1'/0', plus two non-hardened steps. Send the benefactor the 64-hex value. This is the only place this page needs any cryptography, and it is only needed once: derive it, write it down, and you could delete the three imported libraries this page uses for it.

3 · Choose the termsbenefactor

These three values are compiled into the contract, so they also fix its address. They cannot be changed afterwards: a different value is a different, unrelated allowance somewhere else.

Nothing to send yet: the covenant address is part of the terms and does not exist until the allowance is funded, so the handoff is step 5.

4 · Fundbenefactor

Humid builds and signs this from the manifest and spends from its own balance, so fund Humid first: ask liquidtestnet.com/faucet for testnet coins and wait about a minute. Step 2 derives the receive address to give it, and “Check Humid's balance” there confirms they arrived. Dry run first: it costs nothing and proves the manifest and contract pair up.

Humid's receive address, from step 2: not derived yet

5 · Hand the terms overbenefactor

Now that the covenant address exists, the terms are complete and the beneficiary can use them. Send this however you like, since none of it is secret. They paste it into step 6.

6 · Receive the termsbeneficiary

For the two-party case: paste the blob a benefactor sent you from their step 5, and this fills in everything above at once, the three terms and the covenant address, so you can watch and withdraw. Playing both roles in this one browser? Skip this; you already have all of it.

This unpacks rather than discovers. With no compiler here, the address it names is taken on trust: nothing in this page can confirm those three values produce it.

7 · Withdrawbeneficiary

Every UTXO at the covenant address is a separate allowance with its own timelock, never summed and never mergeable. Each is labelled with the action its current balance makes valid.

The timelock measures only the age of that UTXO: the contract counts blocks since whichever transaction created it, and cannot tell, or care, whether that was the benefactor's deposit or the continuation output of your own last withdrawal. So the clock restarts after every partial withdrawal, and an instance you just created waits exactly as long as a freshly deposited one.

Refresh is manual on purpose: this is a shared public Esplora instance that rate-limits hard after a burst.

Left unchecked, Humid builds and signs the withdrawal and sends nothing, the same meaning as the box in step 4. Worth doing once per allowance.
